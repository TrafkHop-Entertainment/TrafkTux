Wichtige neue Information, die meine bisherige Theorie umwirft: Wenn CPU auch bei komplett geschlossenen Widgets bei 100% bleibt, kann das nicht (nur) an neu erstellten Threads pro Timer-Tick liegen — ohne offenes Widget sollte kein einziger Timer laufen. Replace per-call thread creation with a small bounded thread pool. Creating a brand new OS thread for every single periodic timer tick (system monitor every 2s, battery every 10s, media every 1.5s, clock every 1s, etc.) across potentially many simultaneously-open widgets causes exactly the kind of futex/clock_gettime-dominated syscall storm seen in the strace output — CPython's GIL machinery has non-trivial per-thread-creation overhead. A small reused worker pool eliminates that churn while keeping the exact same fire-and-forget call signature everywhere in the codebase.
